Wireframes & Mockups Notes

Design Approach

• Low-fidelity wireframes created in Figma and captured via screenshots.

• Compiled into a single PDF for easy viewing: StudyLink-Wireframes-v1.pdf

• Focus: Simple, intuitive layout for freshmen to submit tutoring requests, and straightforward browsing experience for senior students.

Wireframe List

1. Login Page: Secure student email login form.

2. Post Tutoring Request Form: Clear input fields with placeholders and required field indicators.

3. Public Requests Board: Card-based list with search, filters, and prominent "Assign Myself" button.

All designs align with Use Case Diagram and Product Backlog. Future v2 may add annotations or mobile adjustments.
