# Product Vision: StudyLink - Peer-to-Peer Tutoring Assistance Platform

**For** Taylor's University freshman students  
**Who** are struggling to adapt to university-level studies, facing difficulties in understanding course content like UML diagrams, programming logic, or business case analysis, and missing immediate, approchable guidance from seniors or peers who've been in their shoes.

**The** StudyLink platform  
**Is a** simple web-based public request board  
**That** allows freshmen to post specific academic help requests (e.g., subject code like "ITS6102", topic like "UML Use Case Diagram", description of their confusion, and preferred tutoring time), while also letting them see which seniors have volunteered to help and when they’re available.

**Unlike** official university tutoring services (which may have limited weekly slots, long waiting lists, and require formal bookings through the university portal, long waiting times, or formal booking processes), StudyLink is free, instant, student-to-student, and builds a supportive community among Taylor's students  

**Supporting SDG 4: Quality Education**  
This solution promotes inclusive and equitable access to learning support (Target 4.3), enhances skill development through peer knowledge sharing (Target 4.4), and helps reduce academic stress during the critical first-year transition period.

**Key Justification**  
From our team’s interviews and observations at Taylor's University, many freshmen report feeling overwhelmed by the transition to university. International students, in particular, face additional barriers due to language and cultural differences. Official support channels are often overstretched, leaving a gap that peer tutoring can fill. By creating a platform where seniors can volunteer their time and expertise, we’re not only improving academic outcomes but also fostering a stronger, more connected student community that supports one another’s success.
